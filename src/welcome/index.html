Bandcamp Tab Merger

Every Bandcamp tab you have open, on one page, with one player.

How it works

  1. Open some releases Browse Bandcamp like you normally do and leave the albums you want to hear open in tabs.
  2. Click the extension icon It builds a merged page from every open Bandcamp tab. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so it's always one click away.
  3. Press play One player runs straight through the whole queue. Come back any time, the page keeps itself in sync with your tabs.

What it can do

Grid or list

Browse covers, or switch to a dense list with resizable, reorderable columns and a lazily computed BPM per track.

One queue

Play one track per release or full albums. Drag cards to reorder, expand any release to jump to a specific track.

Sleeping tabs still play

Tabs your browser has put to sleep show up and stay playable. The extension never wakes a tab just to read it.

Tab-aware

Jump to a release's tab, close it (it stays playable from cache), reopen it, or archive closed tabs out of the way.

Listen to a whole label

On any artist or label page a Listen to all button appears above the release grid. It loads the entire catalogue into the same player.

Nothing leaves your browser

No account, no analytics, no server. It streams the same public files Bandcamp's own player uses.
